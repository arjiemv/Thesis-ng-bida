PUP Computer Engineering Thesis Project

Sustainable Campus Hydration with Smart Edge Governance

A self-sustaining, solar-powered thermoelectric water-cooling system equipped with RFID-based fair-share allocation rules. Designed to provide chilled, safe drinking water while managing campus resource distribution efficiently offline.

View Live Telemetry → Administrator Login →
01

Solar Thermoelectric Cooling

Powered by renewable solar energy and MPPT charging controllers, maintaining an optimal chilled reservoir temperature.

02

RFID Fair-Share Governance

Equipped with MFRC522 readers to enforce equitable volumetric limits (500mL per tap) and user cooldown timers.

03

Edge Local Area Network

Operates completely offline over a secure LAN architecture, logging transactions locally on the microcontroller.
